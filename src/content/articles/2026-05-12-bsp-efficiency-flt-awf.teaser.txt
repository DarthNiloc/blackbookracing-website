📊 *New article — The flat-market mirror*

The chase market pays +26% blind because it's thinly traded.

Flat turf? Back every horse, lose −1.6%. All-weather? Lose −4.8%. That's the signature of an efficient market — sharp money has already done the arbitrage work.

Which means the rules for reading flat-racing tipster numbers are the inverse of the rules for chases: on flat, a +5% ROI is real edge. On chases, a +20% ROI is below baseline.

This is why we report flat numbers raw and chase numbers as lift over baseline. The yardstick has to match the market.
