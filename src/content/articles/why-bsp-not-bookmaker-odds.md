Most racing tipster ledgers quote bookmaker odds. The pick goes up at "10/1 with Bet365", and when it wins you see a 10-point profit logged.

The problem is that you can't always get the odds you read on the page. Bookmakers cut prices the moment a tipster service shouts about a horse. Accounts get restricted. Bets get refused, partially matched, or quietly closed-out. By the time the average reader has read the tip and clicked through, the price has moved.

We don't quote bookmaker odds at all. Every settled pick on our ledger uses **Betfair Starting Price** — the price the horse paid on the Betfair exchange at the moment the race went off.

There are three reasons.

## 1. BSP is the price you'd actually have got

Betfair publishes BSP transparently after every race. It's the volume-weighted average of all matched bets in the closing seconds before the off. Anyone can place a Back-SP order in advance and have it filled at that exact number — restricted account or not, retail bettor or not.

If our ledger says +6.41pt at BSP 7.54, that's the number a real punter would have got placing the same bet at the same time, within rounding. There is no version of this where we got better odds than the reader.

## 2. It includes commission

Betfair takes a cut of winning bets — historically 5%, currently 2% on the discount-rate accounts most regular punters use. Our profit figures are post-commission. A pick that returns +1.00pt on the ledger has already had the exchange's slice taken off the top.

Bookmaker tipsters don't have this problem because the bookmaker's edge is built into the price. But it means their gross-of-margin numbers aren't comparable to a punter's net-of-commission reality. Ours are.

## 3. It can't be retro-fitted

A bookmaker quote is a moving target. By the time a result is in, the odds you saw an hour before are gone — you have to take someone's word for what they were. BSP is published as part of the race result. It's a single number, by the regulator-equivalent, and it doesn't change.

When we record a settled pick at BSP 7.54, that number is in Betfair's public race result for the next decade. Nobody has to take our word for it.

## What this means for our ROI

The trade-off is honesty over flattery. Quoting bookmaker odds would let us put bigger numbers on the ledger — wins at top-of-the-market prices, losses at restricted-account prices. Quoting BSP gives a single, audited, replicable number for every pick.

Our ROI is what a punter would have got. The ledger says what the ledger says.
