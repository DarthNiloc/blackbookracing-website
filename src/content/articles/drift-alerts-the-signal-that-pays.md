A horse we've picked at a 4.0 shouldn't be 5.5 in the last hour before the off. When it is, the market is telling us something we missed.

Drift is not always a kill signal — sometimes the market is wrong, sometimes a horse is unfancied for the wrong reasons and goes off at a generous price and wins anyway. But over a few hundred picks, the picks where the price drifts more than a defined threshold into the off behave statistically differently from the picks where the price holds or comes in.

We track this in real time on Blackbook tier and broadcast a Telegram alert when the threshold trips.
