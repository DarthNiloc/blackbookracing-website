We get asked, fairly often, what kind of returns we're projecting. The honest answer is: we don't, and we won't.

There are four things we deliberately don't do, and we think being clear about them up front saves everyone time.

## We don't claim guaranteed returns

UK racing is a parimutuel-flavoured market with thousands of variables we can model and a handful we can't. We've built a methodology that has held up across decades of historical data and a few months of live operation, and the public ledger is what it is.

What it is not is a forward-looking promise. We don't quote target ROIs. We don't put "expected monthly return" on the page. The ledger tells you what happened. The future will look like the future.

## We don't hide losing months

Plenty of tipster services let their losing months fall off the page — the tracker resets, the "since 2024" headline stays the same, the bad weeks vanish. The ledger we publish is cumulative. Every settled pick is on it.

If our overall ROI drops one month because we backed a string of losers, that's what you see on the ledger. We'd rather you trust the number than be charmed by it.

## We don't pool money

We sell information. You read the picks, you place your own bets at your own broker, you keep your own winnings. We don't collect funds from members. We don't manage anyone else's stakes. We don't run a syndicate.

This is partly philosophy and partly law: pooling subscriber money into a common fund managed by us would be a Collective Investment Scheme under FSMA s.235, which is a financial product we are emphatically not authorised to sell.

## We don't sell systems we wouldn't bet ourselves

The picks we publish are the picks we bet. The selections that clear our threshold for a 1-point note on the public Telegram channel are the same selections we'd back with our own money at the same prices. There is no second product we keep for the inner circle — the highest-conviction tier sees the most we have, and we bet from the same slate.

This is partly why our ledger is short and our slate is small. We'd rather skip a day than pad it.

---

What we will do is post the picks before the off, reconcile every result to BSP, publish the losers next to the winners, and let the public ledger be the proof. That's the entire premise.
